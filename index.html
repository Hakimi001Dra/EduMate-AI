For Researchers

Submit Your Research

We welcome original research from Nigerian and international scholars.

  1. Manuscripts must be original and not under consideration elsewhere
  2. Each research paper should not exceed 6,000 words
  3. Prepare in Microsoft Word, Times New Roman, 12-point font, double-spaced
  4. Include an abstract of no more than 250 words with five keywords
  5. References and citations should follow APA 7th Edition style
  6. Provide author details on a separate title page to allow blind peer review
  7. All submissions undergo a double-blind peer review process
  8. Manuscripts must be free of plagiarism (maximum similarity index: 15%)
  9. Disclose any use of AI tools in manuscript preparation
  10. Submit by the deadline shown above to the editorial office
